DLAB

DLAB, Demonstrator Lab Amsterdam, is the incubator of Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.

Shortly after the conceiving of Olivabot, we joined. They helped a lot in the beginning with office space and materials for prototyping, together with business support for planning and development.

From that office came Gibbon Bot, Mycosense — Juan from Mycofarming walked in and asked if we wanted to make water sensors — and Madglove Sense, which started in the same VU ecosystem, DLAB and StartHub.

If you are a student or PhD at the universities of Amsterdam, reach out to them. They might be able to support your entrepreneurial idea.
