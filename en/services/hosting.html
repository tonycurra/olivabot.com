We keep it online, in Europe

Building the thing is the easy half. Somebody has to renew the certificate, apply the security patch, notice the disk is full, and be able to restore last night's database. Olivabot B.V. (Amsterdam) does that on a dedicated server in the European Union. Email for a quote.

What you get every month

  • A dedicated EU server — not a shared box, not a US region. Your users' data stays in Europe.
  • Backups that have been restored — nightly, encrypted, and tested. A backup nobody has restored is a rumour.
  • Security updates — operating system and dependencies patched, HTTPS certificates renewed before they expire.
  • Uptime monitoring — we find out it is down before your customer emails you.
  • Email support on working days — a person who already knows how your system is built.
  • A processing agreement — we can sign one, because we are holding your data on your behalf.

What it is not

It is not new features. When you want the product to do something it did not do before, that is a separate milestone, quoted before we touch it. It is not a 24/7 phone line with an on-call rota, and we will not pretend otherwise. It is not GDPR compliance — see GDPR consulting for what a technical review can and cannot do.

Run it yourself, or let us?

Run it yourself

  • You have someone who enjoys servers
  • You want the credentials in your name only
  • Downtime for a day is survivable

Let us run it

  • Nobody on the team wants the pager
  • Real customers depend on it staying up
  • Data has to stay in the EU and be explainable

How we charge

A monthly fee for a small app on one server. More if it needs a GPU, heavy storage, or several machines — we tell you the number before you commit. Email services@olivabot.com for a quote.

Monthly, cancel with one month's notice. You own the application. If you leave, we move it to a server in your name and hand over the keys rather than holding it hostage.

Ask us to run it

A few lines to services@olivabot.com: what the app does, roughly how many users, whether it needs a GPU, and who runs it today.

Usually paired with: EU web app · computer vision · local LLM agents · GDPR

Short answers

What does the monthly fee cover?
EU server, HTTPS, tested nightly backups, monitoring, security updates, email support on working days. Not new features.
Where is the server?
In the EU. Nothing goes to US AI vendors unless you ask. We can sign a processing agreement.
Am I locked in?
No. You own the app. Monthly, one month's notice, and we hand over the keys.
Will you host something you did not build?
Sometimes. Reading the code and the deployment first is a written assessment. If it is not safe to run, we say so.
GDPR-proof?
No. Data can stay in Europe; you still own compliance. See GDPR consulting.
