Robot learning in Amsterdam

You have a robot, or a simulated robot, and a task it does not do yet. Olivabot B.V. in Amsterdam trains that behavior in simulation and hands over the policy and the training setup, so your engineers can keep going. Remote. Dutch invoice.

  • What you get. A policy for one defined task, the simulator setup, and a short note on how to run it. You own the files.
  • The task. Get up, carry, recover from a shove, use the hands, or move a policy onto a robot that is slightly different from the public model. Flat-ground walking is already on the manufacturer’s channel, so that is not the job.
  • Time and price. About three weeks, €8,000–15,000. Half before the work starts.

Who this is for

Funded humanoid and robot teams, labs, and companies in Amsterdam and the EU that already bought a robot. An open stack with a public simulator, such as Unitree in MuJoCo, or the model you already use. This is an invoice for a result, not a seat on the team.

What sits behind the offer

Simulation and learning on our own machines through 2026, including Gibbon Bot. Gibbon is research. It is not a harvest robot for sale. A model that already runs on a real device is the Madglove glove. A robot practiced in a virtual olive grove is a separate offer: grove training.
