Firmware that wastes less energy and compute

Olivabot B.V. (Amsterdam) writes and reviews firmware for devices that have to last: sleep, radios, sensors, on-device AI. Remote. We do not assemble the box.

  • Battery life, heat, and CPU — what to run on the chip, what to leave off, when to wake the radio.
  • Same problems as Gibbon Bot (ESP32 / nRF, BLE, long battery), Madglove Sense (tiny batteries on a finger), Mycosense (always-on LoRaWAN), and edge vision (small models, thermal budget).
  • Radios in software: BLE, Wi-Fi, LoRaWAN (always-on outdoor sensors, like Mycosense). On-device ML when the model must stay on the chip.
  • Robot control software if you already have the arms — not a field-built machine.
