Why fixed fees and no day rate

A day rate prices our time. You do not want our time — you want the device to stop dying in three days, or the ledger to stop drifting, or a thing your customers can log into. Those are worth what they are worth, and it has nothing to do with how many days we spend.

It also puts the risk in the right place. On a fixed fee, if the work takes longer than we thought, that is our problem and not a bigger invoice. You know the number on day one and it does not move unless you change what you asked for. Nobody counts hours, nobody sends a timesheet, and nobody has an argument at the end of the month.

If we do not think we can price something honestly yet, we will say so and propose the €500 assessment first, so you buy a small answer before a big commitment.

Frequently Asked Questions
Do you work with non-technical teams?
Yes. We help non-technical founders get software they can show: a site, a model, a dashboard, CAD files.
How fast can you deliver?
An assessment usually happens within a week. Projects take one to two weeks for a first version, riskiest parts first. We reply to every enquiry within two working days. We do not build physical hardware as a service.
How does the process work?
Email us your idea. We discuss requirements and send a quote for that scope. First milestone (detailed project plan with risks) is paid upfront. Then we proceed with development, focusing on the most uncertain parts first.
What's your payment model?
Assessments (€500) are paid before the call. Projects are a fixed fee agreed before we start: part upfront, the rest when you can use the result. Hosting and maintenance is monthly, from €150 excl. VAT, cancellable with a month’s notice. No timesheets, ever.
Are these prices real, or a starting point?
Real. An assessment is exactly €500 and a deep assessment exactly €2,500. Project work lands in the €5,000–€10,000 band and the number is fixed in writing before starting; if your scope is bigger than that, we say so and quote it as a larger engagement instead of quietly billing more. The fee does not move because the work took us longer than expected.
Do you handle cloud and data security?
We can host in the EU and sign a processing agreement if we hold the data. That is not “GDPR done.” You still own compliance; a lawyer covers the legal side. See the data and privacy review.
Do you build devices and 3D-print parts?
No. Firmware, CAD (including files for 3D print), IoT software, and dashboards — yes. Assembling boxes or running a printer — no.
Who is your ideal client?
EU teams who need a senior pair of hands for days or weeks rather than a permanent hire — firmware, fintech, software, CAD, or a short consulting call. Not a workshop to mill a first robot, and not a body shop.
