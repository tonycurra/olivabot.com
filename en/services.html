What we can offer

A simple list of possible services. If it sits between hardware, software, and the field, ask.

  • Hardware–software integration – sensors, firmware, apps, and cloud talking to each other
  • Embedded and battery-powered devices – low power, waterproofing, tiny form factors
  • Computer vision and machine learning – cameras, classification, on-device or cloud AI
  • IoT and dashboards – Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, LoRaWAN, EU cloud, monitoring UIs
  • Robotics and automation – locomotion, grippers, control, field prototypes
  • 3D printing and mechanical design – casings, mounts, custom parts
  • Design for manufacturing – help non-technical teams talk to suppliers and DFM partners
  • Feasibility and architecture – what to build first, what not to build, where the risk is

Frequently Asked Questions
Do you work with non-technical teams?
Yes. We specialize in helping non-technical founders turn ideas into working prototypes they can test with customers.
How fast can you deliver?
We deliver working prototypes in weeks, not months. We start with the riskiest parts first to validate feasibility quickly and iterate.
How does the process work?
Email us your idea. We discuss requirements and budget. First milestone (detailed project plan with risks) is paid upfront. Then we proceed with development, focusing on the most uncertain parts first.
What's your payment model?
Fixed price for prototypes. 30-50% upfront to cover costs, balance on delivery. For ongoing software services (cloud, maintenance): pay-as-you-go, monthly, or yearly retainer.
Do you handle cloud and data security?
Yes. EU cloud infrastructure (Scaleway partner), GDPR compliance, IP protection, cybersecurity audits. We can develop quantum-powered applications (experimental) with quantum tunnels for robotic decision-making.
What types of devices can you build?
Interconnected, solar/battery-powered, AI cameras, EU cloud database, cybersecurity audits. Hardware + software + AI + computer vision + machine learning solutions.
Who is your ideal client?
Non-technical startups or SMEs in EU/Amsterdam, pre-seed to seed stage, with customer interest and needing a prototype to demonstrate feasibility. We prefer short, flexible projects and work alone or with our in-house team.
