Frequently Asked Questions
Do you work with non-technical teams?
Yes. We help non-technical founders get software they can show: a site, a model, a dashboard, CAD files.
How fast can you deliver?
Software first versions in weeks, not months. Riskiest parts first. We do not build physical hardware as a service.
How does the process work?
Email us your idea. We discuss requirements and send a quote for that scope. First milestone (detailed project plan with risks) is paid upfront. Then we proceed with development, focusing on the most uncertain parts first.
What's your payment model?
Quote after a short email — not a public price list. Software work is usually a fixed quote: part upfront, the rest when you can use it. Hosting and maintenance, if we run the server: monthly or a retainer.
Do you handle cloud and data security?
We can host in the EU and sign a processing agreement if we hold the data. That is not “GDPR done.” You still own compliance; a lawyer covers the legal side. See GDPR consulting.
Do you build devices and 3D-print parts?
No. Firmware, CAD (including files for 3D print), IoT software, and dashboards — yes. Assembling boxes or running a printer — no.
Who is your ideal client?
Teams in the EU who need software, firmware, CAD, or a short consulting call — not a workshop to mill a first robot.
