VU StartHub

VU StartHub is the startup pavilion on the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam campus. Olivabot’s business address is here: De Boelelaan 1095a, 1081 HV Amsterdam.

We started at DLAB, Demonstrator Lab Amsterdam — the VU incubator for early ideas. Then we moved to StartHub. Same campus, same entrepreneurial environment. DLAB is where you shape the idea. StartHub is where registered VU-linked companies sit.

We have never left the VU Amsterdam entrepreneurial environment. If you are a VU student, researcher, alumnus, or staff with a company, StartHub is the place to ask.
