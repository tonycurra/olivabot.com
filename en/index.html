Research and experimental development

Gibbon Bot is a robot bio-inspired by the gibbon, an arboreal ape — not a bipedal humanoid. The goal is canopy work (maintenance, picking, pruning, inspection, hail nets, monitoring) without compacting the soil. It does not work yet. Laboratory development since May 2025. Not a product, not for sale yet. In a few years, if funding allows and the research progresses, we might put it on the market.
