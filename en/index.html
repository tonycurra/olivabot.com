Firmware, fintech, and software engineering consultancy in Amsterdam

Olivabot B.V. is a small engineering consultancy in Amsterdam that takes short engagements. Firmware for devices that have to sip a battery, engineering for fintech teams with a speed or correctness problem, and web apps and computer vision on a server in the EU. Remote across the European Union, invoiced from a Dutch BV.

We price outcomes, not hours. No day rate, no timesheet. Start with a €500 assessment — a call and a written diagnosis you can act on — or bring us a defined piece of work for a fixed €5,000–€10,000. Larger engagements are quoted as one fee against an agreed result, from €10,000. All excluding VAT, all agreed before anything starts.

We also build our own robots

Gibbon Bot is an arboreal robot that works in the tree canopy instead of compacting the soil under it — monitoring, pruning, inspection. Nobody paid us to build it, which is exactly why it is worth showing you.

The firmware, the battery budget, the vision models, the CAD, and the control software are all ours. That is the same work we do for clients, done on a machine that has to survive a real olive grove rather than a demo. If you want the reasoning behind it, read the arboreal robots R&D notes or the Olivabot Vision feasibility study.

Is this you?

  • A hardware startup that needs firmware, sensing, or vision, but not another full-time engineer yet.
  • A fintech team with something too slow, something that has to be exactly right, or customer data sitting outside the EU.
  • Anyone with one specific broken thing — a driver, a battery budget, a bottleneck — who wants it fixed this month, not scoped for a quarter.
  • A non-technical founder who needs something real to show investors, users, or a pilot customer.
  • A grower or agritech team weighing up a robot purchase and wanting an answer that is not a vendor pitch.

If it is a call, code, or CAD files, ask. If it needs a workshop, a production line, or a crew in a field, we will tell you honestly that it is not us.
