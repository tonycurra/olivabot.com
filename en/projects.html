Research and experimental development

Scientific research and experimental development. Gibbon Bot is a gibbon-inspired arboreal robot. It does not work yet. Laboratory development since May 2025. In a few years, if funding allows and the research progresses, we might put it on the market. Basil Bot is a printed 4-axis arm that tries to see and cut basil leaves. In 2025 we supervised twelve Mechanical Engineering bachelor assignments from the VU–UT programme. We flew a multispectral survey drone over groves and orchards. The engineering is the same work we sell.

Looking for work we did for paying clients instead? That is on client work. Services: firmware, software, consulting.
