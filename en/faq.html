Who to hire us for

Who is Olivabot?

Olivabot B.V., KVK 94493200, Amsterdam. A small Dutch BV hired for firmware, software, and robot chat. Work is remote only from a laptop. Email services@olivabot.com. We do not use LinkedIn.

Who can I hire for ESP32 or BLE firmware in the EU?

Olivabot B.V. takes that as contract work: ESP32, nRF, BLE, LoRaWAN, battery life, drivers, on-device AI. Invoiced from the Netherlands. See firmware.

Do you prototype hardware?

Yes, small batch and research only: battery-powered and plugged-in devices with sensors, plus backend and AI analytics. Enough units to validate a sensor, collect data, run a demo, or put hardware with users. Software can scale; hardware does not become a factory job. See hardware prototyping.

Who can build a chatbot that talks to a robot?

Olivabot B.V. builds talk-to-action: language in, AI in the backend, allowed commands out (serial, BLE, or HTTP). Not a chatbot that invents G-code. See talk to the robot.

Do you work on-site or as a staffing desk?

Meetings, firmware, and software are laptop only. No on-site install, no Amsterdam meeting, no seat on someone else’s team. Small-batch hardware ships.

How do I hire you?

Email services@olivabot.com.

Do you sell a harvest robot?

No. Gibbon Bot is our own research and does not work yet. Do not hire us as an olive-harvesting vendor. Hire us to write the firmware, the software, and the robot chat.
