About Olivabot

Olivabot B.V. is an engineering consultancy in Amsterdam. We do two things, and they feed each other.

We are hired to build things

Firmware for devices that have to survive on a battery, computer vision that turns cameras into usable data, and web applications that keep customer data on a server in the European Union. Clients are hardware startups, agritech and medtech teams, and founders who need something real rather than a slide deck. Work is remote across the EU and invoiced from a Dutch BV.

Everything is a fixed price agreed before we start: a €500 consulting call with a written report, €5,000–€10,000 for a first working version, €5,000–€10,000 for a fixed-scope piece of firmware and CAD, and from €150 per month to keep it running. See all services and prices, or read what we built for Madglove and Mycofarming.

We build our own robots

We are building arboreal robots — machines that work in the tree canopy instead of compacting the soil under it. We started with olives. We believe in sustainable, efficient, data-driven farming that respects both the environment and the people who work in it, and in tools that empower farmers rather than replace them.

Nobody pays us for that work, which is precisely why it is worth showing. The firmware, batteries, vision models, and CAD behind Gibbon Bot are the same skills we sell, proven on a machine that has to survive a real grove. See projects.

What we are not

Not a workshop, not a factory, and not a field crew. We deliver calls, code, and design files. If a job needs someone to assemble boxes, run a printer, or drive a tractor, we will tell you that in the first reply.

DLAB

Olivabot started at DLAB, Demonstrator Lab Amsterdam — the incubator of Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. Shortly after the company was conceived they helped with office space, materials for prototyping, and business support.
