About Olivabot

Our Mission

Olivabot develops robotic solutions for agriculture and forestry. We started with olives. We are building arboreal robots — machines that work in the canopy instead of compacting soil. We believe in sustainable, efficient, and data-driven farming that respects both the environment and the people who work in it.

Our technology bridges the gap between traditional agricultural knowledge and cutting-edge robotics, creating tools that empower farmers rather than replace them.

We also help growers and agritech teams choose the right farm robot for their needs: a 45-minute call and a written report (€500 excl. VAT).

DLAB

Olivabot started at DLAB, Demonstrator Lab Amsterdam — the incubator of Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. Shortly after the company was conceived they helped with office space, materials for prototyping, and business support.
